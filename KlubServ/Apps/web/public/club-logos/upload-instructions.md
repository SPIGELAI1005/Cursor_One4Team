# Club Logo Images Upload Instructions

## Required Images

Please save the club logo images you provided to this directory with these exact names:

1. **tsv-allach-09.jpg** - TSV Allach 09 (Circular green and white logo with four-leaf clover)
2. **fc-grun-weiss-grobenzell.jpg** - FC Grün-Weiss Grobenzell (Shield-shaped logo with green/white sections and football)
3. **fc-augsburg.jpg** - FC Augsburg (Shield with FCA, pinecone, and 1907)
4. **1860-munchen.jpg** - 1860 München (Octagonal logo with lion rampant and 1860)

## Image Specifications

- **Format**: JPG, PNG, or WebP
- **Size**: Recommended 200x200px or larger (will be scaled down to 48x48px)
- **Background**: Transparent or white background preferred
- **Quality**: High quality, clear logos
